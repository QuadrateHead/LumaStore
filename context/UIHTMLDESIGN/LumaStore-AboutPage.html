Tech that earns its place on your desk

LumaStore started with one cluttered desk and a simple question: why is it so hard to find gear that is both well made and well designed?

Our story

We began in 2022 as two friends swapping keyboard and headphone recommendations. Friends started asking us to order for them, and a small shelf of tested products became a store.

Today we curate audio, keyboards, smart watches, and workspace essentials. Every product is tested by our team before it goes on sale, and we drop anything we would not use ourselves.

Our mission

Make it easy to build a workspace you enjoy: fewer, better products, clear pricing, and support from people who know what they are selling.
