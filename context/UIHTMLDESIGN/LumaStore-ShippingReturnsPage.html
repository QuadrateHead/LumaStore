Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day ship the same day. You'll get a tracking number by email once your order leaves our warehouse.

Return policy

You can return any item within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories. Opened headphones and wearables are accepted if they're in like-new condition.

How to return an item

  1. Start a return. Open Order history in your account and choose Return on the order.
  2. Print the label. We email a prepaid label for orders shipped within your country.
  3. Send it back. Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier.
  4. Get your refund. We refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving it.

Refund rules

Refunds cover the item price and standard shipping. Express and pickup fees are not refunded unless the return is due to our error. Discounts applied with a coupon reduce the refund by the same amount. Gift orders are refunded as store credit.

Damaged or wrong item

Contact us within 7 days of delivery. Email support@lumastore.com with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, and cover the return cost.
